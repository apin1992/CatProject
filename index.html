About Us

Dower House Cat Sanctuary resides in the fine city of Eloy, Arizona, very close to Casa Grande and Arizona City. We also routinely travel to Tucson and the Phoenix Metro area.

In fact, our founding board members can be found all over Arizona. We all met twenty years ago as young college kids, and even then our President Sherri had an affinity for cats.

Our mission, in our own words.

Our mission is to reduce the number of cats that are euthanized at shelters due to overcrowding. Our goal is to take cats that would be on Euthanasia lists, and give them a place to learn socialization skills... We do not use a cage system, we use an open home environment to foster a sense of calm, caring and community.

Frequently Asked Questions (Click to reveal)

What is the adoption fee? ▼
Adoption fees and included care will be confirmed by our team. Current fees are awaiting confirmation; no fee is quoted on this website.
Can I meet a cat before adopting? ▼
Absolutely. Send an application and we'll get in touch to arrange a meeting. Visits are coordinated with the sanctuary team.
What if I already have pets? ▼
Tell us about everyone in your household. We'll discuss each cat's needs and help plan a gentle, gradual introduction.
Do you have cats with special needs? ▼
Every cat deserves love. Talk with us about health needs, ongoing care, and whether your home is a good match.
